# The seven references, shot by shot

Paraphrased beats (not the original scripts) with what was on screen. Times in seconds. Use them as worked
examples of how narration turns into pictures. "pull" = snap pull-out, "push" = eased push-in.

## 3. THE BRAVE PASSENGER (52.6 s, 33 cuts) - the "crazy" passenger was the only survivor
- 0-3 s: realistic cabin by the lavatory, coral man in tie smoking; a fire explosion (real fire element) bursts.
- 3-9: flight attendant (yellow, brown hair asset, cap, blue dress) orders passengers (pink woman, lavender man
  with beanie and glasses) to stay seated; a white man with kufi + beard ("crazy man") runs to the cockpit and
  screams; smoke and flames fill the frame.
- 10-16: attendant tells him to sit; he pushes her (push pose), she falls (sit_floor).
- 16.5-19: **B&W moment** "no oxygen masks back then": passengers grey, an oxygen-mask prop with a green arrow.
- 19.5-24: green crew member with apron grabs a fire extinguisher prop, sprays the flames (real fire element).
- 24-27: cockpit: green pilots with mustaches; roll tilt for the aggressive manoeuvre; extinguishers fly.
- 30-36: smoke makes people faint; the man crouches low where there is less smoke (crouch, then lying).
- 39.5: photoreal onion field; 41.5 B&W stock still of a plane falling; 43-46 plane lands in the field with
  explosions (real fire); 47-49 ambulance prop arrives, a tiny figure runs out.
- 49.5-52: grey passengers dead; the crouching man - the one who shouted - survived (loop to his crouch).

## 4. HOW WITCHES SURVIVED THE PAST (57.9 s, 33 cuts) - three accused women, three strategies
- Foggy forest river bank; crowd of pastel townsfolk in black hats with canes; a woman tied with rope to a
  post; underwater (dark teal) she sinks; a witch hat appears on "witches".
- Dark room: three accused women chained at a table (introduce each with a push-in).
- Woman 1 hides a float (prop) under her shirt; thrown in (splash foam); floats pretending to be dead; the crowd
  "sees she's a witch" (red-tinted underwater witch shot); stones thrown (rock prop); "didn't survive".
- Woman 2 (white body, blond hair): a friend jumps into the river with a knife to cut her ropes (underwater
  swimming poses); a guard in a black hood dives after him (knife); both drown -> grey freeze "together".
- Woman 3 (yellow, brown hair): picks stones, hides them in her shoes (shoe props pop); thrown in, sinks faster
  (RED "others"); waits underwater ("30" big caption); lets go of the shoes and resurfaces; crowd apologises.
- Ends on the crowd at the river.

## 5. HE SNEAKED INTO AN ASYLUM (57.5 s, 40 cuts) - the healthy man and the 40 "fakes"
- Red-walled hall: patients in paper party hats and flower crowns, a white doctor with surgical cap and blue apron;
  a teal man (black hair, tie) pretends to be insane; B&W freeze on "lying".
- Sepia ward: he tells the doctors he hears voices (a faded red "voice" figure behind him); doctor diagnoses;
  a medication cart pops; he hides the pills and spits them (eat/drink poses); walks the halls chatting normally.
- "a clear sign he was insane": stronger medication -> **three carts pop in sequence**.
- He reveals he is a doctor (holds a paper) -> grey freeze "everyone fired"; head nurse (pink, nurse cap) challenges.
- Red hall with patients ("send more people undercover"); grey freeze; "after one week": green patient + doctor;
  "40 new people" crowd; grey "weren't insane".
- Window corridor: the nurse proudly shows the results (paper); he smirks: he sent no one -> grey crowd, fade to black.

## 6. HIV OUTBREAK THEORY (54.8 s, 27 cuts) - from a hunter's wound to a pandemic
- Jungle path: white caveman (spiky hair, tunic costume) with a spear chases a chimpanzee cut-out; the chimp
  scratches him (red flash on the character, wound mark); he throws the spear.
- Huts + campfire: cutting meat (meat prop); "chimpanzee's blood" red tint; virus icons appear on his body.
- Bloodstream macro (red, blurred cells): white blood cell, green virus icons circle it; a spiky white immune
  cell; the virus changes colour (mutates) -> **pixel glitch** -> blue viruses multiply (3 copies).
- He turns into a danger (sick); sits by the fire: "normal sickness" -> silhouette with a virus icon on the chest;
  a purple-tinted man passes it on; "100 years later" he walks again; glitch "HIV"; viruses "all continents".
- Dark hall with many tiny multicolour figures lying down ("40 million died"); silhouette by the fire; fade to black.

## 7. MONEY RAIN (51 s, 32 cuts) - a man throws cash off a roof
